KNT VPS Backup

KNT VPS Backup is a private tool the owner uses to keep encrypted recovery copies of their own server on Google Drive. It encrypts and checks snapshots before treating them as usable for recovery.

This page describes the tool. It does not provide access to the server, backups, or any login.

See the privacy policy and terms of use.
